Afghani Pizza

Description:

Afghani Pizza is a uniquely delicious fusion that adapts classic Italian-American pizza to suit
local palates and traditional cooking methods. Often baked in intensive wood-fired brick ovens or specialized
electric pizza decks, it is characterized by its generous toppings, vibrant spicing, and a crust that strikes a
perfect balance between crispy and soft.

The standout feature of pizza in Afghanistan is the creative use of regional flavors. Classic tomato sauces are
enhanced with local herbs, and standard toppings are replaced by intensely flavorful, marinated meats like Chicken
Tikka, Beef Seekh Kebab, or spicy Lamb Chunks, all blanketed under a thick layer of gooey, melted mozzarella cheese.

  • The Cheese & Veggies

Step-by-step Cooking Guide:

  1. Activate the Yeast:

    Dissolve the sugar and yeast in warm water. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes
    until it becomes frothy and bubbly.

  2. Make the Dough:

    In a large bowl, mix the flour and salt. Pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil.
    Knead the dough for about 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover with a damp cloth and let it rise in a warm
    place for 1 to 2 hours, or until doubled in size.

  3. Simmer the Sauce:

    While the dough rises, heat a splash of oil in a small saucepan. Sauté the
    minced garlic for 30 seconds, then pour in the tomato puree, oregano, chili flakes, ground coriander, and salt.
    Simmer on low heat for 15 minutes until thickened, then let it cool.

  4. Shape the Crust:

    Preheat your oven to its absolute maximum temperature—ideally 250°C (480°F)
    or higher—with a pizza stone or baking sheet inside. Punch down the risen dough and roll or stretch it out into
    a 12-inch circle on a piece of parchment paper.

  5. Assemble the Pizza:

    Spread a generous layer of the cooled pizza sauce over the dough, leaving
    a small border for the crust. Sprinkle half of the mozzarella cheese, arrange your cooked Chicken Tikka or beef chunks
    evenly, add the sliced peppers and onions, and top with the remaining cheese.

  6. Bake to Perfection:

    Carefully slide the pizza (with the parchment paper) onto the hot pizza
    stone or baking sheet. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden brown and crispy, and the cheese is
    bubbling and slightly browned. Slice and serve hot!
